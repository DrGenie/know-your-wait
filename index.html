Good to know

  • You usually need a plan from your GP to get a Medicare rebate.
  • Some places are bulk-billed. That means you pay nothing.
  • Some places give you a set date. Others say "sometime in the next few weeks".

          The research

          This tool is part of a University of Newcastle study. We want to know how much people value knowing their appointment date, and the best way to show waiting times.

          The numbers below are made up (simulated). They show what the study will report. They are not results.

          Key numbers

          About the study

          • 200 adults will do an online survey. Each person picks between two appointments, 8 times.
          • 40 people will do the same tasks in our eye-tracking lab, so we can see what they look at.
          • Half see waiting times in words and half see a picture. We check which is easier to understand.
          • Team: Dr Mesfin Genie (lead), Prof Francesco Paolucci, Prof Brendan Boyle, Dr Elaine Xu and Dr Ryan Gallagher.
